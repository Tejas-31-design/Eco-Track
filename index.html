About EcoTrack

Built for People Who Care About Tomorrow

EcoTrack was created with a simple belief: making sustainable choices should be easy, rewarding, and transparent. We built a platform where you can see the real impact of your daily decisions — no jargon, no guilt.

Our carbon calculations use peer-reviewed emission factors from the IPCC and EPA. Every number you see is real and science-backed. We never show fake or estimated statistics.

Whether you're just starting your eco journey or already living green, EcoTrack grows with you. Small daily habits really do add up to a healthier planet.
